Arrest Records is not just a label; it is a tactical strike. In a digital landscape saturated with "low vibration" noise and hollow echoes, we build high-octane, radio-ready Hip-Hop and Electronic hybrids that deliver Biblical truth without compromise. Our standard is absolute: 100% CLEAN. 100% HARD. We believe the "Only Voice" should be broadcasted at the highest possible frequency—with heavy 808-drive and 4K cinematic clarity.

02. THE DIRECTOR: WAKING FROM THE DAD CAVE

Directed and Produced by Nicholas Elia, an established visionary operating from the "Dad Cave" in South Florida. Nicholas is redefining modern trap-hybrids by merging aggressive, modern production with a relentless message of resilience. This project is the culmination of a lifelong journey from the shadows into the Light, proving that technical mastery has no expiration date.

03. THE STORY: THE CRASH & THE ROCK

The journey of Static & Gold was born from the wreckage of a high-speed collision. After a life-altering crash leveled everything I had built on sand, the machine fever broke. In the silence of the aftermath, I found the Solid Ground. We realized that our scars are not flaws—they are Kintsugi, the places where the Divine Gold settles to make us stronger than we were before we broke. Arrest Records is the sound of that restoration.

Bridging the gap between technical mastery (The Scholar) and raw survival (The Shadow).
