The project

Nicholas Elia is a recording artist, director and producer. He builds Arrest Records from the "Dad Cave" in South Florida, making high-octane hip-hop and electronic music you can play in the car, at the gym or in the office.

ARRESTED follows Static & Gold, the 4K visual odyssey that grew the Arrest Records Resistance to more than 2,300 members. The new album carries the same standard forward: radio-ready production, honest lyrics and cinematic visuals. Tracks, artwork and release details will be announced here first. Until then, Static & Gold is streaming on Spotify, Apple Music and Amazon Music.
