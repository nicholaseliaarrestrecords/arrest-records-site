The Concept

To be arrested is to be stopped in your tracks. In the digital world it is a glitch in the system. In the spiritual world it is the moment the static clears and the Original Voice finally gets through. ARRESTED is the sound of that moment: 100% clean, 100% hard.

Nicholas Elia is a recording artist, director and producer. He builds Arrest Records from the "Dad Cave" in South Florida, turning a hard road into high-octane hip-hop and electronic music you can play in the car, at the gym or in the office.

ARRESTED follows Static & Gold, the 4K visual odyssey that grew the Arrest Records Resistance to more than 2,300 members. The new album carries the same standard forward: radio-ready production, honest lyrics and cinematic visuals.

Tracks, artwork and release details will be announced here first. Until then, Static & Gold is streaming everywhere.
