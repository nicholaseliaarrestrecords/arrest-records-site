About Nicholas Elia

Nicholas Elia is a hip-hop and electronic recording artist, director and producer, and the founder of Arrest Records, an independent label in South Florida. His music is 100% clean and 100% hard: radio-ready tracks with heavy 808 drive and a message of resilience, paired with cinematic 4K visuals he directs himself.

His latest project, Static & Gold, is streaming now, and his next album, ARRESTED, arrives 1st quarter 2027.
